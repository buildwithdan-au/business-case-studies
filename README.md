# 📁 Case Study Portfolio — Dan McHugh

A collection of interview case studies, take-home exercises, and self-directed teardowns spanning **revenue strategy**, **digital banking**, **SaaS partner programs**, **product growth**, and **B2B marketing**.

> Together they show a consistent approach:
> **frame the problem → ground it in data → stress-test the recommendation → land it with a clear execution plan.**

<br>

---

## 🧭 How I approach a business problem

Across every case in this set, the same operating pattern shows up:

<br>

**1. Start with the hypothesis, not the data dump.**
Each deck opens with a clear recommendation or hypothesis before walking through supporting evidence.
> e.g. the Xero Partner Program redesign leads with a testable hypothesis on growth vs. Rule of 40, rather than burying it in analysis.

<br>

**2. Quantify wherever possible.**
Growth assumptions are broken into named, falsifiable drivers.
> e.g. Asana's FY22 forecast splits +34% YoY growth into SDR-led, marketing-led, and seller-efficacy components — each with its own %.

<br>

**3. Segment before recommending.**
Customer, partner, or market segments are defined by specific, measurable traits rather than intuition.
> e.g. CBA's "InvestLab" cohort, defined by income, balance, channel preference, and an interest score.

<br>

**4. Be honest about weak signals.**
Several decks explicitly flag where correlation is weak or data is unrepresentative, rather than overstating confidence.
> e.g. CBA: R² = 0.02 on income vs. interest score — recommendation framed as "pilot and validate," not "roll out."

<br>

**5. Close with execution, ownership, and governance.**
Nearly every case ends in a phased plan with named milestones, a RACI/governance structure, or a "fast start" plan.
> The thinking isn't complete until it's operational — e.g. a 30/60/90 plan, or a Month 0–3 rollout timeline.

## 📚 Case studies

<br>

### 1. Asana APJ RevStrat
**Files:** `Asana_APJ_RevStrat_Case_Study.pdf` · `Asana_APJ_RevStrat_Case_Datapack.xlsx`

**Problem:** Build an FY22 revenue forecast (+34% YoY) across 7 regional teams and justify the AE hiring plan, marketing investment, and SDR build-out needed to hit it.

**Approach:** Decomposed growth into investment-led, seller-efficacy, and market assumptions; built a team × lead-source funnel model; used FY21 close-rate and deal-size data to prioritize where an outbound SDR motion would have the highest ROI.

<br>

### 2. CBA Digital Banking ("InvestLab")
**Files:** `CBA_Digital_Banking_Case_Study.pdf` · `CBA_Digital_Banking_Datapack.xlsx`

**Problem:** Identify which customer segments are most likely to adopt a new in-app financial planning tool.

**Approach:** Ran correlation analysis across income, balance, digital engagement, and churn; called out weak/misleading signals in the dataset; defined a 26-customer top-quartile pilot cohort (interest score 0.93 vs. 0.56 average) and a phased pilot-to-national rollout plan with explicit go/no-go gates.

<br>

### 3. Xero Partner Program Redesign
**File:** `Xero_CommOps_Case_Study.pdf`

**Problem:** Redesign the Partner Program tiering and benefits structure to outgrow FY25's 22% YoY and protect Rule of 40.

**Approach:** Rebuilt tiers around points-based outcomes (Customer Delight, Growth, Accelerate, Scale) rather than volume; modeled High/Medium/Low uplift scenarios with explicit ARPU and sub-growth assumptions; flagged blind spots (partner segmentation, long-tail churn, ecosystem tie-ins) before proposing a governance structure and 3-month implementation timeline.

<br>

### 4. Salesforce Sales Strategy Director Panel
**File:** `SFDC_SalesStrat_Case_Study.pdf`

**Problem:** A market-entry business case — which APAC market should Salesforce enter next?

**Approach:** Compared 5 candidate countries on TAM, digital competitiveness, talent availability, and regulatory/hyperscaler environment; recommended Malaysia over Thailand despite Thailand's higher CAGR, based on the weight of secondary factors; paired the business case with a 30/60/90 fast-start plan for the role itself.

<br>

### 5. golf.ai Marketing Plan
**File:** `Golf_ai_Marketing_Case_Study.pdf`

**Problem:** Scale an AI golf-coaching app to 500K users.

**Approach:** Mapped the full acquisition-to-monetization funnel (trial → exploration → community → integrations) against user psychology at each stage; phased the growth plan (Early Adopters → Expansion → Mass Adoption) with channel and monetization strategy tied to each phase.

<br>

### 6. EducatorData.com UX Teardown
**File:** `EducatorData_com_Case_Study.pdf`

**Problem:** Unsolicited product improvement pitch — reduce friction and increase engagement on a B2B insights platform.

**Approach:** Identified 4 concrete UX changes (personas, JTBD-based customer journeys, removing dead dashboard views, opening up voting visibility to free users); for each, laid out current state → proposed state → expected impact → internal feasibility considerations, then sequenced them into a 3-phase growth structure.

| **Commercial & quantitative fluency** | Comfortable building forecast models, correlation analysis, and TAM/CAGR comparisons — not just narrative slides |
